# Never lose your train of thought again.

Arova remembers:
• **what you were doing**
• **why you were doing it**
• **what decisions you already made**
• **what you should do next**

---

## Why Arova?

Every morning when you open your laptop, you pay a 20–30 minute **Context Reconstruction Tax**:
> *"Where did I stop yesterday? Which file was open? Why did I bookmark this documentation? Why did we decide against using that database?"*

Arova is **Persistent Working Memory** for knowledge work. It runs quietly on your desktop, observes your work across files, documentation, and tools, and synthesizes an objective understanding of your momentum. 

When you sit down to work, Arova gives you back your morning:
- **Zero-Hunt Resumption**: Pick up any project exactly where your mind left it with a single **[Resume →]** click.
- **Decision Continuity**: Automatically surfaces settled decisions (*"You already decided Tier 3 requires explicit consent"*) so you never waste hours debating old arguments.
- **Active Environment Preparation**: Reopens exact files, browser tabs, and terminal directories automatically.
- **Quiet, High-Signal Interventions**: Intervenes rarely—only when it observes repeated friction or loops—delivering constructive analytical guidance rather than generic chatbot prompts.

## Phase 0 — Ship Beta (Productization Roadmap)

We have frozen core architectural expansion to focus 100% on **Productization & Design Partner Validation**.

### Week 1: One-Click Installer & Single Process
- **Single Executable**: Double-click (`Arova.dmg` / `Arova.exe` / `Arova.AppImage`).
- **Daemon Auto-Start & Recovery**: Launching Arova automatically manages the background runtime (`arovad`), local SQLite stores, and auto-restarts on failure. Zero terminal interaction required.
- **First-Run Onboarding**: 3-step setup (*Welcome → Choose Provider: OpenAI / Groq / Ollama → Continue*).

### Week 2: Public Repository Polish & Diagnostics
- **Debug Report Export**: One-click local diagnostic bundle export for effortless troubleshooting.
- **Documentation & License Governance**: Clear setup guides, contributing standards, and legal/license structure (`Business Source License` / `PolyForm Noncommercial`).

### Week 3: Design Partner Daily Validation
- Sitting with **5 daily Design Partners** across Engineering, Writing, Research, and Founder workflows.
- Tracking the single metric that matters: *"Would you be annoyed if I uninstalled this today?"* and verifying weekly **Context Recovery Time Saved**.
